How to Go From Wallet Setup to a Filled Order

Cowswap guide: connect a wallet, approve a sell token, sign the order, then verify its filled status and received amount by order ID in CoW Explorer.

By Blockchain Today EditorialNov 19, 2026 · 12:07 UTC5 min read

Connect your wallet to cowswap, choose a network and token pair, approve the token if needed, and sign the order. Solvers compete to settle that signed intent, and CoW Explorer lets you verify the result by order ID.

  • Start with a wallet funded on a supported network, the token you want to sell, and native tokens for approval gas.
  • Review the quote, approve the sell token when required, then sign the order.
  • Look up the order ID in CoW Explorer and confirm its status and received amount.

A CoW Swap order is a signed message, rather than a transaction you submit to execute the trade yourself. To get started with cowswap, use cowswap; the site gives you a way to place the swap described here.

What do I need before starting a cowswap?

Before placing an order, make sure your wallet holds the sell token and is connected to the network you intend to use. Ethereum, Gnosis Chain, Arbitrum One, and Base are network names to look for when choosing a network for your trade. The wallet also needs the network’s native token if the first sale requires an approval transaction.

Select the network and the token pair, then enter the amount to sell. Review the quote before proceeding: network costs are taken from the sell token and included in the quote. That lets you assess the expected amount in the receiving token alongside the amount leaving your wallet.

Check the token addresses and network in your wallet before signing anything. A token’s name or symbol alone may not identify the asset you intend to trade. Keep the order ID when it becomes available; you will use it to check the outcome.

How do I approve and sign the swap?

For a first sale of an ERC-20 token, the wallet usually asks you to approve its use. Approval is an on-chain transaction, so it costs native gas. Tokens with permit support may let you approve with a signature instead. Approval authorizes use of the sell token; it is separate from placing the swap order.

Once an ERC-20 sell token is already approved, signing the order costs no gas. The signature records your trade intent and its conditions. Solvers compete in a batch auction, then submit the settlement on-chain and pay its gas. That cost is recovered from the sell amount and included in the quote.

Selling native ETH has an extra distinction: it needs an on-chain transaction through the Eth-flow contract. An already-approved ERC-20 sale needs only the order signature. So the wallet activity differs before the order is settled: an approval transaction may be needed for a first ERC-20 sale, while native ETH uses the Eth-flow transaction.

After signing, the order is open for solvers to execute under its conditions. The settlement changes the token balances: the wallet gives up the sell amount and receives the bought token. A fill can deliver more than the quote because surplus from solver competition goes to the trader.

Limit and TWAP orders are other order types available in the CoW Swap context, but this walkthrough follows a single swap from wallet connection through settlement.

How do I confirm the order filled, and what if it stays open?

Open CoW Explorer and search for the order ID. A filled status and the received amount confirm the result. Compare the amount received with the quote you reviewed; a higher amount can reflect surplus returned from solver competition.

Before the trade, note the sell amount and the quoted receive amount. After a fill, check that the sell token decreased and the receiving token increased by the amount shown. The order status and amount explain what changed; the approval, if one was needed, only authorized token use and did not itself make the swap.

An order that remains open may have an expiry that is too short, slippage set too tightly, or a missing approval. Look up its status in CoW Explorer, then review the order conditions and approval state in your wallet. A pending order is not a confirmed fill, so use the Explorer status rather than assuming the trade completed.

The signals to watch are the order status, the received amount, and whether the approval and order conditions match your intent. Those checks distinguish a completed swap from an order that still needs attention.

Common questions about cowswap

Is cowswap safe to use?

Review the network, token, amount, and order conditions before approving or signing. A signature places the order intent; CoW Explorer lets you check whether it was filled and what amount arrived.

Does cowswap charge a fee?

Network costs are included in the quote and taken from the sell token. Signing an order costs no gas; solvers pay settlement gas and recover it from the sell amount. An approval transaction, when required, uses native gas.

How do I cancel a cowswap order?

For an open order, use cowswap to cancel it if cancellation is available for that order, and review the wallet request before signing. Check the order ID in CoW Explorer afterward to confirm its status.
