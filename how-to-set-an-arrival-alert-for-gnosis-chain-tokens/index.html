How to Set an Arrival Alert for Gnosis Chain Tokens

Learn how to get notified when a transfer arrives on Gnosis Chain: add the receiving address to a GnosisScan Watch List and enable ERC-20 token alerts.

By Blockchain Today EditorialNov 25, 2026 · 10:13 UTC4 min read

To get notified when a transfer arrives on Gnosis Chain, add the receiving address to a GnosisScan Watch List and enable email alerts for incoming transactions and ERC-20 token transfers. You need a free GnosisScan account and an email address. The token-transfer setting matters because an Omnibridge delivery is executed by Bridge validators, not by the recipient.

  • Add the Gnosis Chain receiving address to your GnosisScan Watch List.
  • Choose incoming notifications and tick ERC-20 token-transfer tracking.
  • Use the email’s transaction hash to check the delivered token and amount on GnosisScan.

Address-level alerts can replace repeatedly refreshing a bridge status page when you are waiting for tokens to land. For a transfer from Ethereum, bridge to gnosis chain is a concrete case: Gnosis Bridge moves tokens between Ethereum and Gnosis Chain, and a GnosisScan Watch List can email you when the receiving address is credited.

Token-transfer tracking catches validator deliveries

For an ERC-20 token bridged to Gnosis Chain through Omnibridge, enable token-transfer tracking because a Bridge validator sends the delivery transaction. The recipient’s address is credited inside that transaction; it does not send the transaction itself.

That distinction affects which alert setting can catch the arrival. An incoming-transaction alert watches transactions associated with the address, but the delivery can be an ERC-20 transfer recorded within a validator’s transaction. The ERC-20 token-transfer option tracks that token movement to the address. With only incoming-transaction notifications selected, the alert can miss the delivery.

GnosisScan’s Watch List offers email alerts to users with a free account. The explorer sees transactions that credit the address, including bridge deliveries, so the watch list can notify you without relying on you to keep checking the status page. The trade-off is that the alert tells you a transfer reached the address; you still need to inspect the delivery transaction to confirm the token and amount.

Add the receiving address and choose both alert settings

Set up the GnosisScan Watch List entry with the Gnosis Chain address that should receive the ERC-20 tokens, then enable incoming alerts and token-transfer tracking.

  1. Start with the receiving address. Have the address that you supplied as the destination for the bridge transfer. Make sure it is the Gnosis Chain receiving address you want monitored.
  2. Sign in to GnosisScan. Use your free account, with the email address where you want arrival notifications sent.
  3. Open Watch List and click Add. Paste the receiving address and add a note that will help you recognise it in your list. The note is for identifying the address; the address itself determines which credits the watch list monitors.
  4. Select incoming-transaction notifications. This is the general alert for incoming activity associated with the address. Keep going to enable the specific token tracking needed for a bridged ERC-20 delivery.
  5. Tick ERC-20 token-transfer tracking. This is the key setting for the validator-executed delivery. The validator sends the transaction, while the token transfer within it credits your address. Tracking ERC-20 transfers lets the alert cover that credit even though you did not send the transaction.
  6. Save the entry. The receiving address is now on your Watch List with both incoming notifications and token-transfer tracking selected. The next time an ERC-20 transfer credits it, GnosisScan can send an email.

Use the email hash to confirm the delivery

When tokens land, the GnosisScan alert email carries the delivery transaction hash; open that transaction on GnosisScan to verify what arrived.

Use the hash from the email to locate the transaction, then check that it records a token transfer to your receiving address. Confirm the token and amount against the transfer you were expecting. This gives you a transaction-level check of the arrival rather than relying only on the notification.

The setup is complete when the next transfer lands and its alert arrives by email. If you are still waiting, the useful signal is the delivery transaction appearing for the receiving address: the email supplies its hash, and the transaction lets you confirm the token and amount.
