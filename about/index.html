About Blockchain Today

Blockchain Today reports on the crypto industry as a whole: networks and protocol upgrades, exchanges and stablecoins, decentralised finance, security incidents, and the policy and regulation shaping the market. Each story sets a development against what came before and against comparable projects, so the change and its trade-offs are clear.

Reporting draws on primary sources such as filings, code repositories, official statements and on-chain records. When an error is confirmed, the article is corrected and the correction is noted. Blockchain Today is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
