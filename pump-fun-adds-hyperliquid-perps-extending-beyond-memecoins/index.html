Pump.fun adds Hyperliquid perps, extending beyond memecoins

Pump.fun has added Hyperliquid-routed perps to its app, widening its trading offer while leaving execution, collateral and liquidation with Hyperliquid.

By Blockchain Today EditorialOct 18, 2026 · 08:10 UTC3 min read

Pump.fun has added perpetual futures trading to its app, routing orders to Hyperliquid and extending the Solana memecoin platform into leveraged markets. TokenPost’s October 16 report says Hyperliquid executes and settles trades and holds users’ positions and collateral. Pump.fun supplies the interface; the exchange runs the trade.

The feature brings Bitcoin and Ethereum contracts, along with contracts tied to real-world assets including the S&P 500 and oil, according to AMBCrypto’s report on the rollout. It reports leverage of up to 50x across the offerings. AMBCrypto said the feature was live but that Pump.fun or its leadership had not made a separate official announcement as of October 17. The reported launch is therefore clearest as a product change visible in the app, rather than a publicly explained token or revenue strategy.

What changes when Hyperliquid handles the trades?

Users can reach perpetual markets through Pump.fun, but the underlying trade remains on Hyperliquid. TokenPost reports that users submit signed orders through Pump.fun, while Hyperliquid matches and executes them and holds the resulting position and collateral. This separates the customer-facing platform from the venue providing the trading infrastructure.

That arrangement brings a derivatives product into the same app used for creating and trading tokens. But it also means that access through Pump.fun does not put trade execution under Pump.fun’s control. TokenPost reports that users may lose all the collateral they post if a position is liquidated, and that prices, margin requirements or liquidation levels shown in Pump.fun may differ from Hyperliquid’s execution. It also says open positions may remain on Hyperliquid if access through Pump.fun ends.

The launch adds Pump.fun to a field of consumer platforms that use existing venues to provide perps. TokenPost points to Fomo, which launched perpetuals powered by Hyperliquid and Trade[XYZ] in June 2026. AMBCrypto also names Phantom, MetaMask and Rabbit Wallet among platforms offering leveraged trading through Hyperliquid. For users, the practical comparison is where they prefer to access a trade; the reports do not establish that Pump.fun offers a different execution venue or a distinct market structure.

Does the integration give PUMP or HYPE a direct lift?

The product launch does not, by itself, establish a direct link between trading activity and either token. More trades routed to Hyperliquid could mean more activity for its venue, but whether Pump.fun brings incremental traders or shifts existing volume is not yet clear. The sources reviewed do not specify a fee-sharing arrangement or say that perps revenue will fund PUMP purchases or HYPE buybacks.

That leaves the token question open. Pump.fun’s expansion gives users another reason to trade inside its app; Hyperliquid provides the execution and settlement. Whether that translates into recurring volume or token demand depends on usage and economics that the launch reports have not confirmed. The next signals to watch are sustained perps volume through Pump.fun, the range of supported markets and any published details on fees or revenue allocation. Until then, the integration is a confirmed product expansion, not evidence of a price catalyst for PUMP or HYPE.
